About

I am an Information Technology undergraduate student (Cybersecurity track) at Vinh Long University of Technology Education (VLUTE), expecting to graduate in late 2026.

My research trajectory bridges physical IoT systems and trustworthy empirical machine learning:

  • Edge Telemetry & Traceability: Designed and deployed an outdoor cellular telemetry system for durian orchards, producing traceable sensor snapshots with operational metadata (undergraduate thesis, advisor: Tran Hoai Hanh, M.Sc., score 8.9 / 10, project: AgriFusion-IoT).
  • Tabular ML & Weak Supervision: Co-authored an empirical benchmarking study on foundation models (TabPFN, FT-Transformer) versus gradient boosting on weak rule-derived agricultural IoT targets (submitted to EAI AICON 2026; under review).
  • Benchmark Validity: Currently leading a first-author study on observation contracts and effective evidence formulations to evaluate target leakage, temporal history, and distribution shift in IoT telemetry.
